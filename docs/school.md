# School

Industry `school` inside the CloudTech Business Suite: a basic school management system. It reuses tenancy, roles, subscriptions, audit and the shared
expenses module. Migration: `supabase/migrations/20261021000019_school.sql`. Demo mirror: `src/demo/demoSchool.ts`.

## What it does

| Area | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kind of school | Nursery only, primary only, secondary only, all, **online**, or **Montessori**. Only that kind of school's classes and subjects exist and are shown; a class of another stage is refused, and narrowing the school is refused while pupils are still in the stages it would hide |
| Setup | "Start from the standard structure" creates classes for the school type, three terms, subjects, grading scale and 40/60 marks; everything is editable |
| Students | Admission numbers are generated and never reused; class history is kept as enrolments; leaving is a status (transferred, withdrawn, graduated), never a delete |
| Admissions | Shown as the Applications tab on the Students page. Applications are accepted or declined; accepting creates the student, links the parent and raises the admission bill in one step |
| Parents | Guardians link to students; a parent's login is matched by email the first time they open the school |
| Attendance | Per class and date; no future days, non-school days or holidays; corrections are audited; teachers only their own classes |
| Results | Class-work and exam marks validated against the school's maximums; draft, submitted, approved, published; report cards print; remarks and promotion decision |
| Fees | Fee items, bills per student and term (re-running skips existing bills), discounts and extra charges, partial payments capped at the balance, receipts, reversals with a reason |
| Teachers | **My Classes** (their own page): the classes and subjects assigned to them, with today's register status and results status per subject. Open a class for its pupils (today's mark, attendance rate this term, parent name and phone) and a printable class list. Row-level security already limits every row to their own classes |
| Parents | The **Parent Portal** has six tabs: Overview (attendance rate, absences, fees owing, latest notices), Attendance (a month calendar), Results (report card with printing, plus the average for every term), Fees (each bill with items, payments and receipts, printable statement), Notices, and My details (keep their own phone and address up to date) |
| Timetable | School managers set the periods of the school day and, class by class, the subject and teacher for every period of every school day. The database refuses a lesson in a break, on a non-school day, for a subject the class does not take, by a teacher who is not assigned to it, or with a teacher in two classes at once. Teachers see their own **My week** on My Classes; a parent sees their child's class timetable in the portal |
| Set up at registration | Choosing the kind of school when registering the business builds its classes, terms, grading scale, departments and subjects for that kind straight away, with no sample students or fees |
| Classes & Subjects page | Three steps on one page: 1. Classes (grouped by nursery, primary, junior and senior secondary; open one to tick its subjects), 2. Subjects (each listed once, with the levels and department it is for), then departments, terms and holidays. Classes, subjects and departments can all be deleted when unused; a class with students, attendance, results or fees (or a subject with results) is refused, and a class can be marked not in use instead |
| Changing the kind of school | Saving a different kind in School Settings reshapes the school: going from secondary to primary or nursery removes the departments and the unused secondary classes and subjects, and adds the primary or nursery classes and subjects, ready to adjust. Classes holding records are kept as not in use; it is refused while pupils are still in a class that would go |
| Online school | A school that offers courses instead of classes. It starts with no courses: the owner or an admin adds each course with a title, duration and description, and adds its units. A course works like a class everywhere else: learners enrol in it, instructors are assigned to it, and attendance, results, report cards and fees (set a fee per course) all apply. It runs in **cohorts** (intakes such as "January 2027 intake") instead of First, Second and Third Term: adding a cohort sets up its single period automatically, and every term picker says Cohort. Sessions, cohorts and terms can be deleted when unused. Pages say Course, Unit and Learner for these schools; "all" never includes online courses |
| Report card extras | Each student is rated 1 to 5 every term on the school's own list of skills (affective: punctuality, neatness and so on; psychomotor: handwriting, sports and so on), entered by the class teacher on the **Skills** tab of Results and printed on the report card. The card also shows the position in each subject (when positions are switched on), a passport photo, and the pupil's rating key. Teachers and the principal pick ready-made comments (editable in School Settings) when writing remarks. An **annual report** adds the terms of a session into one card with the annual average, grade and position; print one, or the whole class from Results |
| Bulk work | **Import CSV** on Students registers many students (with a parent) from a spreadsheet, row by row, skipping and explaining bad rows; the same file twice never registers anyone twice. **End of year** promotes a class in one go: each pupil is set from the decision on their report card (move up, stay, graduate) and can be changed before applying. Scores can be filled from a CSV on the Results page (columns admission_no, ca, exam) and reviewed before saving |
| Photos and documents | Each student has a passport photo (chosen when registering, or added later under Documents; it shows as a round avatar in the student list and on the profile) that is used on the ID card, report card, annual report and transcript. Each student can also have up to 15 uploaded papers (birth certificate, previous results, medical record and so on, about 1.5 MB each; pictures are shrunk for you). Papers are visible only to people who manage students. From a student's Documents tab the school can print a two-sided ID card (front: school colours, large photo, name, class, admission number and session; back: return address, emergency contact and a QR code of the admission number; previewed on screen), an admission letter, a transcript of every published session, and a certificate; **ID cards** on the Students page prints a whole filtered list, ten to a page. Files are kept in the database so the same row-level security protects them; very large scans should not be uploaded |
| Printing | Report cards (one or a whole class) and fee receipts are designed A4 sheets that print on their own, without the app around them. A receipt shows the school, receipt number, who paid for whom, the bill's items, the amount received and the balance, with a REVERSED stamp if the payment was reversed. Receipts print from Payments, from a student's fees and from the parent portal |
| Montessori school | Mixed-age **environments** (Toddler Community 18 months to 3, Children's House 3 to 6, Lower Elementary 6 to 9, Upper Elementary 9 to 12) and the Montessori **areas**: Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Mathematics, Cultural Studies, Art and Music, Grace and Courtesy. Each area has **works** (the standard list is supplied and editable per area). There are no exams, marks or grades: on **Progress & Reports** guides record where each child is on each work (introduced, practising, mastered), write a note per area, and the school prints a narrative **progress report** each term (one child, or a whole environment). Parents see their child's progress report in the portal. Attendance, fees, photos, ID cards and the rest work as in any school; "all" never includes Montessori environments |
| Departments | Senior secondary classes belong to a department (Science, Art, Commercial); schools can add, rename or delete departments (only unused ones can be deleted). Each SS class starts with its department's subjects plus the common ones, then the school picks what each class takes |
| Subjects by level | Each subject is tagged with the levels it is taught at (nursery, primary, junior secondary, senior secondary) and optionally a department. The class subject picker lists subjects that fit the class first and the rest under "Other subjects" |
| Deleting | Owner and business admin can delete a student or a parent/guardian, with a reason. A student's attendance, results and class history go with them; a student with fee payments is refused, since money records are never erased. Everything is audited |
| Report card | Designed for A4: school heading, pupil details, class work/exam/total/grade per subject with class average, highest and lowest, average and position, attendance, remarks, next term date, grading key and signature lines. Print one card, or **Print all report cards** for a class (one per page) |
| Teacher's view | A teacher works only with what is assigned to them: their classes in every class picker, only the subjects they teach in Results, and only their own pupils and those pupils' parents (the **Parents & Guardians** page, read-only). **My Classes** is for teachers only; the owner, admins, managers and the principal see the whole school instead |
| Deleting and suspending staff | The owner or an admin can delete a staff member (reason required, audited): the record and class assignments go, their lessons stay on the timetable without a teacher, and their access to the school is removed (their own account is kept). Staff & Roles also has Delete for any member. A suspended or removed person is locked out of the whole workspace and sees a clear screen saying so, which also appears within a minute if they are already signed in |
| Staff sign-in | A staff record is just a person; cleaners, drivers and security need no login. When adding someone who should sign in, choose **Can sign in**: pick their role (teacher, bursar, admissions officer, principal) and a temporary password (one is generated; the page shows it once with a Copy button). At first sign-in they are made to choose their own password. The email must not already have an account; for someone who does, use the invitation instead (they sign in with their own password). An unused invitation can be withdrawn |
| Reports | Enrolment, attendance, results and fees, each exportable to CSV |
| Announcements | By audience: everyone, parents, teachers, staff or one class |

## Security

* Roles: `principal`, `teacher`, `bursar`, `admissions_officer`, `parent`. The SQL role permissions must equal `src/core/permissions/roles.ts` (checked by `registry-consistency.test.ts`).
* Row-level security: teachers read only students, attendance and scores of classes assigned to them; parents read only their linked children, published results and their own bills and payments; fees need `school.fees.read`.
* There are no direct write grants. Every write goes through a guarded function; money is append-only and corrected by reversal.
* Audit triggers cover students, attendance, scores (including corrections to published results), bills, payments, admissions, settings and staff.

## Tests

`tests/integration/school.test.ts` (database, PGlite) and `tests/integration/demo-school.test.ts` (browser mirror).

## Limitations

No online payments, no email or SMS, minimal holiday handling, one set of grading bands per school, and results positions are optional.
Concurrent payments are guarded by a row lock, but the single-connection test database does not exercise that path.

## Deployment

Apply migrations in order (the school migration is last), then create a business of type School. Invite staff with the roles above; parents are invited
with the `parent` role using the email recorded on their child's guardian record.
